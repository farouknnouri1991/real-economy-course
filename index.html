Clarity is not simplification. It is the point at which complexity becomes intelligible without losing the institutions, histories and conflicts that give it meaning.

The approach

Four lenses, one economy

The course holds competing perspectives in productive tension. Each lens answers a different question; none is treated as a decorative appendix.

01

Mainstream analysis

Models are presented as disciplined tools: their assumptions, mechanisms, uses and limits are made explicit.

02

Heterodox traditions

Power, instability, distribution and structural change enter the analysis as central economic objects.

03

Institutions

Firms, markets, banks and states are studied as concrete organisations shaped by rules and history.

04

Moroccan evidence

Moroccan institutions and development choices anchor the reasoning and open comparative questions.
